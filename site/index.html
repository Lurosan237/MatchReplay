Pick a match: every pass, shot and card comes back in order, and the live numbers (score, expected goals, momentum) update the way a streaming pipeline computes them during a real broadcast. Only here for the technology? Open the Lab.

How it works

The data is Hudl StatsBomb's open event data: every on-ball action of 558 matches, from AFCON 2023 to the World Cups. The pipeline (Java, Apache Flink, a Kafka-compatible broker) receives each match the way a real feed would deliver it, with events late, out of order or twice, and keeps the live numbers right anyway; it is tested against a simple reference, including with a server killed in the middle of a match. This page replays the same numbers in your browser, no server needed. The World Cup 2022 (matches marked 3D) also plays in 3D from real optical tracking: every player and the ball, 30 times a second.

Data: Hudl StatsBomb open data; World Cup 2022 tracking: PFF FC. A student portfolio project, not affiliated with any competition, club or data provider.
