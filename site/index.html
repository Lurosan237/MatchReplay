How it works

The data is Hudl StatsBomb's open event data: every on-ball action of 558 matches, from AFCON 2023 to the World Cups. The pipeline (Java, Apache Flink, a Kafka-compatible broker) receives each match the way a real feed would deliver it, with events late, out of order or twice, and keeps the live numbers right anyway; it is tested against a simple reference, including with a server killed in the middle of a match. This page replays the same numbers in your browser, no server needed. Coming next: live win probability, "what if this shot had gone in?", and the World Cup 2022 in 3D from real player tracking.

Data: Hudl StatsBomb open data. A student portfolio project, not affiliated with any competition, club or data provider.
