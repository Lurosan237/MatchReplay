What you are watching. Every action of this match, replayed in order from the real event data (Hudl StatsBomb open data). The ball moves between the recorded actions; the players themselves are not shown here, because event data says where the ball was, not where everyone stood. The numbers on the right are the snapshots the streaming pipeline emits every 5 seconds of match time (score, shots, expected goals, passes, box entries, momentum), the same ones the Java/Flink job is tested against. Win probability is a model trained on all 558 matches: each team's chance of scoring in every minute left, from the score, the clock, red cards and the chances both teams have created so far, played forward to the final whistle. The Flink job computes the same number live, in the stream. In the match feed, What if? flips one moment (a goal, a missed shot, a red card) and replays the rest of the match with the changed score: how the odds would have read, and thousands of simulated endings. Momentum on the timeline adds up recent threat (the xG of shots, plus a little for every entry into the box), forgetting half of it every 5 minutes: above the line the home team is pushing, below it the away team.
